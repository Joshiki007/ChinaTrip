Day 1 (Nov 2): Full Schedule & Alternatives

12:00 PM – 12:20 PM | Hotel → Yu Garden

Take a DiDi ride (20–35 mins depending on midday traffic) south to Yu Garden.

12:20 PM – 1:45 PM | Yu Garden (豫园)

Explore traditional Ming dynasty pavilions, ponds, and rockeries. Entry ticket is ~40 RMB.

1:45 PM – 2:45 PM | Yuyuan Old Street & Lunch

Browse Old City bazaar streets and Fuyou Market lanes. Grab lunch (soup dumplings at Nanxiang Steamed Bun Shop).

2:45 PM – 3:15 PM | Travel to East Nanjing Road

Take Metro Line 10 (Yuyuan Station to East Nanjing Rd Station) or a short DiDi.

3:15 PM – 4:00 PM | East Nanjing Road Pedestrian Street

Stroll eastward down the bustling shopping boulevard directly toward the waterfront.

4:00 PM – 4:45 PM | The Bund (Daytime)

Take in the historic western colonial facade buildings and look across the Huangpu River to Pudong.

4:45 PM – 5:15 PM | Commuter Public Ferry

Walk to Jinling East Road Ferry Pier and take the 2 RMB (~30 cents) commuter ferry across to Pudong (Lujiazui).

5:30 PM – 7:30 PM | Shanghai Tower Observation Deck

Ascend the world's 2nd tallest building for sunset and night lights. Reserve ticket in advance.

8:00 PM – 9:00 PM | The Bund at Night

Cross back via Metro Line 2 or ferry to view the skyline illuminated before walking/taking DiDi back to hotel.

Day 2 (Nov 3): Full Schedule & Alternatives

9:00 AM – 10:00 AM | Jade Buddha Temple

Active Buddhist temple in Putuo district housing two famous white jade Buddha statues imported from Myanmar.

10:15 AM – 11:30 AM | Tian An 1,000 Trees

Short DiDi/walk away. Futuristic architectural complex planted with 1,000 live trees along Suzhou Creek.

11:30 AM – 12:30 PM | Lunch Break

Eat inside 1000 Trees shopping/bistro hub to avoid burning time traveling for food.

12:30 PM – 1:15 PM | Transit to French Concession

Take a DiDi across town directly to Wukang Mansion to skip multi-transfer metro walks.

1:15 PM – 3:30 PM | Wukang Mansion, Wukang Rd & Anfu Rd

Photograph the iconic 1924 flatiron building, then wander down leafy streets filled with cafes and boutiques.

3:30 PM – Evening | Choose Evening Neighborhood

Option A (Xintiandi): Restored shikumen stone-gate houses with upscale dining patios.
Option B (Tianzifang): Maze of narrow arts-and-crafts alleys with street snacks and bars.

Day 3 (Nov 4): Full Schedule & Alternatives

8:00 AM – 9:30 AM | Hotel → Zhujiajiao Water Town

Head west early via Metro Line 17 or DiDi (60–90 mins) to experience the canals before large tour groups arrive.

9:30 AM – 12:30 PM | Zhujiajiao Ancient Town

Walk over Fangsheng Bridge, take a wooden hand-paddled boat ride, and explore narrow stone pathways.

12:30 PM – 1:30 PM | Canal-side Lunch

Enjoy local specialties (braised pork belly, zongzi dumplings) at a canal-side restaurant.

1:30 PM – 3:00 PM | Return to Central Shanghai

Return via Metro Line 17 or DiDi. Keep afternoon flexible for traffic.

3:00 PM – 4:00 PM | 1933 Old Millfun [Optional]

Gotham-style concrete slaughterhouse turned art hub in Hongkou. Skip if return runs late.

4:30 PM Onward | North Bund & Farewell Dinner

Stroll the tranquil North Bund promenade in your hotel's neighborhood with views of Pudong towers.

Yu Garden & Old Street

💰 ~¥40🕒 2.5 hrs

Built during the Ming Dynasty (1559). Famous for Exquisite Jade Rock, pavilions, and crowded traditional markets surrounding the ponds.

East Nanjing Road

💰 Free🕒 45 mins

Shanghai's main pedestrian commercial street leading directly from People's Square to The Bund.

The Bund & Commuter Ferry

💰 ¥2 Ferry🕒 1 hr

52 European colonial buildings facing the Huangpu River. Take the 2 RMB public ferry from Jinling East Pier across to Pudong.

Shanghai Tower

💰 ~¥180🕒 2 hrs

632-meter skyscraper with the world's fastest elevators taking you to the 118th floor observation deck.

The Bund at Night

💰 Free🕒 1 hr

Lights usually stay on until 10:00 PM. Offers breathtaking contrast between 1920s architecture and futuristic Lujiazui.

Jade Buddha Temple

💰 ~¥20🕒 1 hr

Active Chan Buddhist temple known for its two seated and reclining Burmese white jade Buddha statues.

Tian An 1,000 Trees

💰 Free🕒 1.25 hrs

Designed by Thomas Heatherwick, inspired by China's Yellow Mountain, featuring terraced concrete pillars containing real trees.

Wukang Mansion & Anfu Road

💰 Free🕒 2 hrs

Designed by Laszlo Hudec in 1924. Heart of the Former French Concession, packed with trendy coffee shops and boutiques.

Xintiandi / Tianzifang

💰 Free Area🕒 Evening

Xintiandi offers upscale pedestrian dining in shikumen courtyards; Tianzifang offers narrow, artsy shopping lanes.

Zhujiajiao Water Town

💰 Free Area🕒 4.5 hrs

400-year-old water village on the outskirts of Shanghai featuring 36 stone bridges, waterways, and ancient Ming/Qing houses.

1933 Old Millfun

💰 Free🕒 1 hr

Former municipal abattoir built in 1933 with Art Deco geometric concrete bridges, spiral staircases, and lattice windows.

North Bund Waterfront

💰 Free🕒 Evening

Quiet, modern promenade in Hongkou District with direct views of the Oriental Pearl and Shanghai Tower across the river.
