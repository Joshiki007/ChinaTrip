Day 1: Full Detail Plan

1. 09:00 – 11:30: Dongshankou (东山口)

Explore historic red-brick villas, independent shops, galleries, and cafes.

2. 11:30 – 12:45: Lunch

Eat locally in Dongshankou or nearby Zhujiang New Town.

3. Transfer to Huacheng Square

Take DiDi from Dongshankou; allow roughly 4–6 km and 15–25 minutes, depending on traffic.

4. 13:00 – 14:45: Huacheng Square & Zhujiang New Town (花城广场)

Walk through modern CBD; view Guangzhou Library, Opera House, and skyscrapers from outside.

5. 15:00 – 16:00: Haixinsha / Haixin Bridge Area (海心沙 / 海心桥)

Walk from the Huacheng Square area toward the riverfront and cross Haixin Bridge if open and convenient. The bridge does not end at the Canton Tower entrance; allow extra walking afterward.

6. Transfer to Canton Tower Entrance

Walk the remaining route or use a short DiDi ride. Follow your pre-booked entry time and the tower's current access instructions.

7. 16:15 – 18:15: Canton Tower Observation Decks (广州塔)

Pre-booked sunset entry slot (General operating hours 09:30–22:30).

8. Walk to Canton Tower Fortune Pier

The pier is near Canton Tower at 222 Yuejiang West Road. Follow the exact pier name and check-in instructions printed on your cruise ticket.

9. Evening: Pearl River Night Cruise & Hotel Return

Board cruise at Fortune Pier, then DiDi back to Tianzi Wharf hotel.

Day 2: Full Detail Plan

1. 09:00 – 10:00: Sacred Heart Cathedral (石室圣心大教堂)

Cost: Free | Hours: Saturday morning visiting hours 09:00–11:30.
Explore Gothic granite architecture and stained glass. Enforces dress code (covered shoulders and knees).

2. Transfer to Chen Clan Ancestral Hall

Take DiDi from Sacred Heart Cathedral; allow roughly 3–4 km and 15–25 minutes, depending on traffic.

3. 10:30 – 12:00: Chen Clan Ancestral Hall (陈家祠)

Cost: ~¥10 | Pre-book via official WeChat mini-program (陈家祠).
Explore courtyards, wood carvings, brick carvings, and roof decorations.

4. Transfer to Lunch

Take DiDi from Chen Clan Ancestral Hall toward Pantang / Liwan Lake area; allow roughly 1.5–2.5 km and 10–20 minutes.

5. 12:30 – 13:45: Lunch

Enjoy Cantonese dim sum or local specialties in the Liwan Lake area.

6. 13:45 – 14:45: Pantang Ancient Village & Liwan Lake Park (泮塘古村 & 荔湾湖公园)

Walk through historic lanes, park, ponds, and pavilions.

7. Transfer to Yongqing Fang

From Pantang, take DiDi (roughly 2–3 km; allow 10–20 minutes) or walk if you prefer a longer transfer (around 25–35 minutes, route-dependent).

8. 15:15 – 17:00: Yongqing Fang (永庆坊)

Explore restored Xiguan architecture, canal lanes, boutiques, and Cantonese Opera Museum.

9. Transfer to Shamian Island

Take DiDi from Yongqing Fang; allow roughly 2–3 km and 10–20 minutes, depending on traffic and the chosen drop-off point.

10. 17:15 – 18:30: Shamian Island (沙面岛)

Enjoy historic European-style architecture and tree-lined streets.

11. 18:30 Onward: Dinner & Hotel Return

Eat on Shamian or nearby, then return to hotel at Tianzi Wharf by DiDi.

Day 3: Full Detail Plan

1. DiDi to Baiyun Mountain South Gate

Allow roughly 7–10 km and 20–35 minutes as a planning estimate; traffic and the chosen entrance affect the route.

2. 09:00 – 11:45: Baiyun Mountain (白云山)

Take cable car to Mountaintop Park, then walk/shuttle toward Moxing Ridge summit.

3. 12:00 – 13:00: Lunch

Grab lunch near the mountain base or on the way to Yuexiu Park.

4. Transfer to Yuexiu Park

Take DiDi toward Yuexiu Park; allow roughly 5–7 km and 15–30 minutes.

5. 13:15 – 15:30: Yuexiu Park (越秀公园)

Five Rams Sculpture and Zhenhai Tower / Guangzhou Museum area (North-to-South walk).

6. 15:30 – 16:15: Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall Exterior (Optional)

Include only if feeling energetic after exiting south gate of park.

7. DiDi to Beijing Road Area

Allow roughly 3–5 km and 10–25 minutes as a planning estimate, depending on traffic.

8. 16:30 Onward: Beijing Road Pedestrian Street & Return

Browse shops, see ancient glass-covered road remains, enjoy snacks, walk/DiDi to hotel.
