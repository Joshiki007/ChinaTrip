Day 1: Futian Half-Day

1. 1:30 PM: Huaqiangbei Electronics Market OR MOCAPE

Transit: 1.5–2 km from Hotel. DiDi: 5–8 mins (~¥15) or Walk 18 mins.
Activity: Focus on ONE highlight to avoid rushing.
- Option A (Tech): Explore Huaqiangbei's gadget stalls, drone showrooms, and components markets.
- Option B (Art/Architecture): Visit MOCAPE for futuristic indoor architecture and 3D urban development models.
Alternatives: HQB Museum (华强北博物馆) if at Huaqiangbei, or Shenzhen Library (深圳图书馆) next to MOCAPE.

2. 4:00 PM: Lianhuashan Park

Transit: 1–3.5 km North. DiDi 10 mins (~¥16) or Walk 15 mins.
Activity: Take an unhurried, gentle walk up to the Deng Xiaoping statue plaza to watch the sunset over the Futian skyline and see the city lights turn on across the CBD.
Alternatives: Guan Shanyue Art Museum (关山月美术馆) at the eastern foot of the park if it is too hot/humid outside.

3. 7:00 PM: Zhuoyue Center / One Avenue

Transit: 1.2 km South. DiDi 8 mins (~¥12) or Walk 15 mins down central axis.
Activity: Enjoy a sit-down dinner at your own pace. Stroll the cyberpunk-lit pedestrian strip filled with outdoor seating, massive LED displays, and craft beer spots.
Alternatives: Link City / Central Walk (领展中心城) for underground indoor dining options right nearby.

Day 2: Museum, Arts & Coastal Bay Sunset

1. 10:00 AM: Shenzhen Museum

Transit: 2 km North from Hotel. DiDi 8 mins (~¥15) or Walk 25 mins.
Activity: (Closed Mondays). Explore Shenzhen's rapid transformation in an air-conditioned, low-stress museum. No WeChat reservation needed—just present your physical passport!
Alternatives: MoCAUP (深圳当代艺术与城市规划馆) if you prefer modern art over historical exhibits.

2. 1:00 PM: OCT Loft Creative Culture Park

Transit: 11 km West from Museum. DiDi 20 mins (~¥35) via highway.
Activity: 3.5 whole hours for lunch and unstructured wandering. Enjoy tree-shaded 1980s factory alleyways, indie coffee roasters, street art, and boutique design shops.
Alternatives: MixC World (万象天地), a high-end open-air fashion and architecture plaza 10 minutes away.

3. 5:00 PM: Shenzhen Bay Park & Talent Park

Transit: 6 km South from OCT Loft. DiDi 15 mins (~¥25). Set destination specifically to "深圳湾公园-地铁站D2口".
Activity: Stroll along the coastal promenade during golden hour and sunset with open views of the water, sea breezes, and the Hong Kong skyline across the bay.
Alternatives: Head straight to Sea World (Shekou) early if you want a busier waterfront dining atmosphere.

4. 8:00 PM: OCT Harbour (Bay Plaza)

Transit: 3 km North from Shenzhen Bay. DiDi 8–10 mins (~¥15) or Walk ~35 mins via connected paths.
Activity: End the night at this open-air waterfront complex featuring illuminated lakes, musical fountains, and lakeside dinner spots.
Alternatives: Yitian Holiday Plaza (益田假日广场) for an indoor air-conditioned shopping and dining complex.

Day 3: History & Shekou

1. 9:30 AM: Nantou Ancient City

Transit: 20 km West from Hotel. DiDi 30 mins (~¥45) via Shennan Highway.
Activity: Explore a 1,700-year-old historic fortress town. Features ancient city gates, Lingnan architecture, and indie craft shops built right into historic brick alleyways. Grab lunch here.
Alternatives: Zhongshan Park (中山公园), Shenzhen's oldest public park located right next door, full of massive banyan trees.

2. 1:00 PM: Nanhai E-Cool

Transit: 11 km South from Nantou. DiDi 25 mins (~¥25) straight down into Shekou.
Activity: A former Sanyo factory complex converted into a shaded, relaxed walking block packed with greenery, cafes, boutiques, and outdoor dining.
Alternatives: Dutch Flower Town (荷兰花卉小镇), a European-themed flower market street located further north.

3. 3:45 PM: Sea World Culture and Arts Center (SWCAC)

Transit: 1 km South from E-Cool. Walk 15 mins along the coastal promenade.
Activity: Minimalist waterfront architectural complex designed by Fumihiko Maki. Walk up to the public roof garden for panoramic views of the sea and mountains.
Alternatives: Nuwa Coastal Park (女娲滨海公园), the open oceanfront path right outside the building.

4. 6:15 PM: Sea World Plaza (Shekou)

Transit: 0.8 km West from SWCAC. Walk 10 mins down the promenade.
Activity: End your trip at Shekou's famous waterfront Plaza centered around the Minghua, a massive real cruise ship permanently docked in the plaza. Watch the free musical fountain show (7 PM & 8 PM).
Alternatives: G&G Creative Community (G&G创意社区), an indie shipping-container lifestyle park 15 minutes away.
