Day 1: Full Detail Plan

1. 8:30 AM – 5:00 PM: Yiwu International Trade City

Transit: 10-minute walk straight from your hotel lobby.
Details: Focus purely on wholesale market business without worrying about commuting or traffic. The market complex is massive, so plan your target districts ahead of time. Wear comfortable shoes and keep supplier contact cards organized.

2. 5:30 PM – 6:30 PM: Dinner

Grab dinner at a nearby restaurant in Futian or head into the central business district for a casual sit-down meal.

3. 6:30 PM – 9:00 PM: Jiming Pavilion (Jiming Mountain)

Cost: Free park admission | Transit: 15-minute DiDi ride from hotel (~20 RMB).
Head up Jiming Mountain as dusk falls to see the multi-tiered Tang/Song dynasty-style pavilion glowing in gold lighting. Walk the paved paths around the peak for panoramic vistas of Yiwu's night skyline.

Day 2: Full Detail Plan

1. 9:30 AM – 11:30 AM: Futian Wetland Park

Cost: Free | Transit: 10 to 15-minute walk from hotel.
Enjoy a slow, effortless morning stroll across lake bridges, lotus ponds, and tree-lined trails to relax and unwind after walking the markets on Day 1.

2. 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM: Lunch

Grab a casual local lunch near your hotel or in the Futian neighborhood.

3. 1:30 PM – 5:30 PM: Fotang Ancient Town

Cost: Free entry | Transit: 35-minute DiDi south (~60 RMB each way).
Explore a preserved 300-year-old riverfront town at your own leisure. Walk cobblestone streets, admire Ming and Qing Dynasty courtyard architecture with intricate wood carvings, and sample local street snacks like Yiwu brown sugar candy (hongshengtang), brown sugar rice cake (hongshenggao), and hand-pulled noodles.

4. 6:00 PM Onward: Return to Yiwu City Center

Transit: 35-minute DiDi back to hotel/city center (~60 RMB).
Head back for a relaxed sit-down dinner at a local Zhejiang restaurant or rest up before your flight/train home.
