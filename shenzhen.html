Day 1: Futian Cyberpunk Tech, History & CBD Skyline

1. 13:30 – 15:30: Huaqiangbei Electronics Market (华强北)

Transit: Eastern Futian starting point (~5–8 min DiDi or 18 min walk from hotel).
Activity: Explore SEG Electronics Plaza and multi-story markets for drones, custom hardware, action cameras, and gadgets.

2. 15:45 – 17:15: Shenzhen Civic Center: Shenzhen Museum (深圳博物馆)

Transit: ~5 min DiDi (~¥15) or Metro Line 2/8 west to Civic Center.
Activity: Visit the Shenzhen Reform & Opening-Up Exhibition inside the East Wing of Civic Center. Learn about Shenzhen's transformation from a rural fishing county into a tech megacity. Bring original physical passport for free entry (last entry 17:30).

3. 17:15 – 19:00: Lotus Hill Park (Lianhuashan Park / 莲花山公园) [MUST VISIT]

Transit: 3 min walk north via connected elevated skybridge over Fuzhong Road.
Activity: Hike 15–20 minutes up the paved path to Deng Xiaoping Statue Plaza for sunset over the lit Futian skyscrapers. Search specifically for 莲花山公园 to avoid map routing errors.

4. 19:15 Onwards: Zhuoyue Center / One Avenue (卓悦中心 / 节日大道)

Transit: ~8 min DiDi (~¥12) or 15 min walk south down central axis.
Activity: Sit-down outdoor dining, craft breweries, and neon pedestrian street walks flanked by high-definition LED street corridors.

Day 2: Nanshan Creative Arts & Coastal Bay Sunset

1. 10:00 – 12:00: OCT Loft (华侨城创意文化园)

Transit: ~15–20 min DiDi (~¥35) west from Futian hotel.
Activity: Quiet morning stroll through converted 1980s factory alleyways, pop-up design shops, open art studios, and street art shaded by banyan trees.

2. 12:00 – 13:15: Lunch Break @ OCT Loft

Transit: Stay within OCT Loft block.
Activity: Relaxed patio lunch at SeeSaw Coffee & Bistro or Gaga Cafe right inside OCT Loft.

3. 13:15 – 15:30: MixC Shenzhen Bay (深圳湾万象城)

Transit: ~15–20 min DiDi (~¥25) south to 深圳湾万象城.
Activity: Cool down indoors during peak sun, grab a dessert/coffee, or browse modern installations at Fotografiska.

4. 15:30 – 18:15: Shenzhen Talent Park & Coastal Promenade (深圳人才公园 & 深圳湾公园)

Transit: Walk 5 mins straight out of MixC into Talent Park.
Activity: Walk the Talent Park lake loop to photograph reflections of the Spring Bamboo tower. Continue onto the open coastal promenade for golden hour, sunset, and views across the bay to Hong Kong.

5. 18:30 Onwards: Houhai CBD Dinner & Return to Futian

Transit: 5–10 min walk via skybridge into dining districts.
Activity: Evening skyline dining at MixC Shenzhen Bay or Coastal City (海岸城) for Cantonese dim sum, hot pot, or international bistros. Take DiDi or Metro Line 11 back to Gangxia North / Futian.

Day 3: Ancient Roots, Coastal Shekou & Early Departure Prep

1. 09:30 – 11:45: Nantou Ancient City (南头古城)

Transit: ~25 min DiDi (~¥45) west from Futian hotel.
Activity: Enter through ancient fortress gates. Browse historical Lingnan architecture, small heritage displays, and stone alleys.

2. 11:45 – 12:30: Lunch @ Nantou Ancient City

Transit: Stay within ancient city.
Activity: Relaxed 45-minute meal inside the renovated ancient town streets.

3. 12:30 – 15:30: Sea World Culture & Arts Center / SWCAC (海上世界文化艺术中心)

Transit: ~25–30 min DiDi (~¥25) south down Shekou peninsula.
Activity: Minimalist coastal building by Fumihiko Maki. Walk up to the public Roof Garden for open sea and mountain views across Shekou port.

4. 15:30 – 17:15: Sea World Plaza & Early Dinner (海上世界广场)

Transit: 10–15 min coastal walk (~800m) west into Sea World Plaza.
Activity: Photograph the permanently docked 1960s Minghua cruise ship and eat an early dinner at an outdoor patio bistro.

5. 17:15 Onwards: Return to Futian Hotel & Early Bed

Transit: ~30 min DiDi (~¥45) back to hotel.
Activity: Return to hotel by 18:00–18:30. Shower, finish packing luggage, set multiple alarms, and rest early for 04:30 AM train.
